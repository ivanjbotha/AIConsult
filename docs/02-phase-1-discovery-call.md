# Phase 1: The Discovery Call

A 45-minute Zoom or Google Meet with the client. This call has one job: **pull
out problems.** You are not pitching anything.

## Setup

- **Record the call.** Have an AI notetaker in the meeting — Fathom, Otter,
  Fireflies, whatever. The transcript it produces is the raw material for
  Phase 2.
- Full question script: [templates/discovery-call-script.md](../templates/discovery-call-script.md).

## The core questions

- "Walk me through your day yesterday."
- "What do you tend to do in a typical business day?"
- "What are tasks in your business that you dread doing?"
- "Where does your work pile up?"
- "What have you tried to automate in the past that failed?"
- The magic wand question: **"If you could wave a magic wand and delete any
  process in your business, what would it be?"**

You'd be shocked how often the answer to the magic wand question is *email* —
"if I never had to answer an email again, I'd be a different person." That's
solvable to a degree with AI, and it's exactly the kind of pain point this
assessment monetizes.

## The one rule: bite your tongue

Most business owners have a lot of the same problems. Within minutes you'll
hear a pain point and know exactly what tool fixes it. **Do not prescribe on the
first call.** The first call is only for probing.

Why:

- Prescribing in the moment gives away the deliverable and undercuts the report.
- The AI analysis in Phase 2 will catch patterns you missed in real time and
  find pain points you didn't think were solvable.
- The structured report — with ROI math and a quick-start plan — lands far
  better than an off-the-cuff tool name.

## Exit criteria

By the end of the call you should have:

- [ ] A recording + transcript from the AI notetaker
- [ ] At least 5 distinct pain points or time drains
- [ ] A rough sense of hours lost per week per pain point
- [ ] The client's approximate hourly value (needed for the ROI slide)
- [ ] Their tech comfort level and current tool stack (needed for QA in Phase 2)

Then move to [Phase 2: AI Analysis](03-phase-2-ai-analysis.md).
