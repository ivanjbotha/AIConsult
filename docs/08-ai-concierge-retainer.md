# The AI Concierge Retainer

The favorite upsell: **done-with-you consulting at an effective $1,000+/hour**,
and the engine of monthly recurring revenue.

Proof of demand from the source playbook: of the first six people pitched,
**five closed** — $8K MRR in the first 10 days, raising the price on every yes
($1,200 → $1,500 → $1,500 → $1,800 → $2,000/month).

## The offer

- **Two 45-minute Zoom calls per month.** The client shares their screen; you
  help them use Claude Cowork and build Claude skills. That's it.
- **Unlimited Voxer (async voice/text) access** with a 12-business-hour
  response time. Sounds heavy; in practice the workload is virtually zero —
  of five clients over ~2.5 months, only three ever messaged, once each. Very
  high *perceived* value, near-zero cost to deliver.

At $1,500/month for two 45-minute calls, your effective rate is **$1,000/hour**.
Not a fan of trading time for money? At $1,000/hour, most people are willing.

## Why it sells after the assessment

The assessment uncovers skills and workflows worth building. The pitch writes
itself:

> "If you want me to do it *with* you, I can be your in-house AI concierge —
> twice a month we do working sessions and build this stuff together."

## Call structure

**Call 1 is always foundation, never building.** Most clients have never used
Claude Cowork (or never set it up properly). Use a guided onboarding — this
playbook uses a Claude plugin that walks the client through it:

- Connect their tools
- Create context files
- Create global instructions
- Show them a scheduled task
- Show them what a skill does

Every call after that is 10× more effective because the foundation exists.

**Every subsequent call: run AOA on one of their workflows.**

| Step | What you do |
|------|-------------|
| **A**udit | "Show me how you do it today" — they walk through it |
| **O**ptimize | Cut the fat — 13 steps becomes 7 |
| **A**utomate | Turn it into a Claude skill |

Rinse and repeat for every workflow, for as long as they're a client.

## Making it feel premium

1. **Pre-engagement onboarding form** (template:
   [templates/concierge-onboarding-form.md](../templates/concierge-onboarding-form.md)) —
   pointed questions, especially: *"90 days from now, what would make this
   engagement feel like a win?"* Get a specific answer. (Four of five clients
   hit their 90-day win inside 60 days — then you remind them of it and upsell
   again. There's always an upsell to the upsell.)
2. **A Notion one-pager per client** where the whole engagement lives — in
   particular, an **inventory of everything you've accomplished together**.
   Update it after every call and email it to them. When renewal time comes,
   "we did this, this, this, and this" is staring them in the face. That's why
   they keep paying.

## Pricing & scarcity

- Ladder the price: quote each new client higher while demand holds
  ($1,200 → $1,500 → $1,800 → $2,000...).
- On a landing page, tier it publicly: "4 spots at $800/month, then $1,200,
  then $1,600" — services need urgency or people don't act.
- **Cap your roster and say so** (this playbook caps at six): "I'm taking one
  more client for this offer and I have two other calls this week. If you say
  no today and one of them says yes, I can't work with you until someone
  churns." It must be legitimate scarcity — and with a capped roster, it is.

## The recurring math

5 clients × $1,200–$2,000/month = **$8K+ MRR** for ten 45-minute calls a month
plus a trickle of Voxer messages. And as clients churn, replace them at a
higher price point — or package something more premium. There's always a more
luxury tier some ICP wants.
