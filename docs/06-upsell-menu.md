# The Upsell Menu

$999 is just the door. Roughly half of assessment clients want implementation —
and they're warm: it's far easier to pitch a $5,000 Claude-skills pack to
someone who just paid you $1,000 than to cold traffic off a Facebook ad.

Six things to sell after the assessment:

## 1. Process Redesign (Process Optimization) — ~$3,000–$3,500

Fix a broken process. No automation, no AI — take a 16-step process and make it
seven. Process-map the current state; the deliverable is the **future-state
blueprint**.

*Example:* an e-commerce seller spent 10 hours/week manually monitoring Amazon
ad spend and wanted to "just automate it." Wrong first move — throw AI at a
broken process and it falls apart. Fix the process first ($3K–$3.5K), then:
"if you want us to automate the new process, that's a separate engagement."

## 2. Simple Automation Build — ~$1,500

Zapier / Make.com / n8n flows. Ideal when there's a cut-and-dry input, a
cut-and-dry output, and 1–3 steps — where a Claude skill would be overkill.

*Example:* a wedding-venue business whose ops manager duplicated the same
Asana project structure for every new client (~30 min/week). One Zap, set up
once, $1,500 — and the process scales with the business. Anybody can build
this after 2 hours of YouTube, or by just asking Claude what to do.

## 3. Knowledge System — priced per build; retainer-friendly

Train an AI on the client's documents so it answers questions for them.

*Example:* a business broker got 400–500 emails per listing, all asking the same
five questions. Build: a custom GPT trained on the listing's marketing package;
interested buyers get the GPT link instead of his email. Result: zero repetitive
emails (efficiency) and both buyer and seller called it "the most interesting
experience we've ever had buying or selling a business" (quality).

## 4. Custom Workflows (Claude Skills) — ~$3,000 per pack

For proprietary processes specific to how the business operates. 90% of the
time the answer is a Claude skill: skills + reference files, train the team,
plug and play.

**Bake in recurring revenue:** operations change constantly. "Pay me $3K to
build skills for your customer onboarding — and a monthly/quarterly retainer to
maintain and update them as your processes change."

## 5. Retainer Packages — recurring versions of any of the above

Turn any one-time fee into MRR once trust is built:

- "$3K/month — up to two knowledge systems per month."
- "$2K/month — up to three automated workflows per month."

Show them one working build first; monthly is much easier to sell after
they've seen results. Size it to the client and their needs.

## 6. Full Implementation — ~$8,000+

Any combination of the above: two processes redesigned + a couple of Claude
skills + a Zapier workflow, packaged as one engagement.

## Picking your lane

Focus is key. Do the assessment (easy to fulfill), then **choose one** upsell
to specialize in — concierge, knowledge systems, or Claude skills. Don't try to
offer everything at once. And remember there's always an upsell to the upsell:
as clients churn and bandwidth frees up, you can keep moving up-market with
more premium packages.
