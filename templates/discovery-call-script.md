# Discovery Call Script (45 minutes)

> **Golden rule: bite your tongue.** You will recognize the fix within minutes.
> Do not prescribe anything on this call. Probe only — the report does the
> prescribing.

**Before the call:** AI notetaker joined and recording (Fathom / Otter /
Fireflies). Confirm with the client that recording is OK.

---

## 1. Opening (5 min)

- "Thanks for making time. Here's how this works: I'm going to ask you a bunch
  of questions about how your business runs day to day. I'm not going to pitch
  or recommend anything today — I'll take everything we discuss, do the
  research, and come back with a written report prescribing 3–7 specific tools.
  If I can't find you at least 5 hours a week, you get your money back."
- Quick context: "Tell me about the business — what do you do, who's on the
  team, roughly what does a year look like?"

## 2. The day-in-the-life walk (10 min)

- "Walk me through your day yesterday, start to finish."
- "What do you tend to do in a typical business day?"
- "How much of your week is spent working *in* the business vs. *on* it?"

Listen for: repetitive admin, context switching, anything they do that an
employee or a tool should be doing.

## 3. Pain mining (15 min)

- "What are tasks in your business that you dread doing?"
- "Where does your work pile up? What's in the backlog right now?"
- "What have you tried to automate or delegate in the past that failed? What
  happened?"
- "What eats your evenings or weekends?"
- "What's the task where, when it shows up, you procrastinate?"
- **The magic wand:** "If you could wave a magic wand and delete any process
  in your business entirely, what would it be?"
  - (Very often the answer is *email*. Dig in: what kinds of email? From whom?
    What are the repetitive ones?)

For each pain point, quantify it:

- "How many hours a week does that take?"
- "Who does it — you or someone on the team?"
- "What does it cost you when it goes wrong?"

## 4. Stack and comfort level (10 min)

- "What software do you use today, tool by tool? CRM? Accounting? Scheduling?
  Project management?"
- "Have you used ChatGPT or Claude? For what?"
- "On a 1–10, how comfortable are you adopting a new tool?"
- "Any tools you've bought and abandoned? Why?"
- "Roughly what's your time worth per hour?" (Needed for the ROI slide — if
  they don't know, estimate from revenue ÷ owner hours.)

## 5. Close (5 min)

- "Anything we haven't covered that frustrates you weekly?"
- Set expectations: "You'll get the report by [date]. Read it before our review
  call — we'll walk through every recommendation together, and each one will
  have the cost, the setup time, and the hours it gives you back."
- Book the review call before hanging up.

---

## Post-call checklist

- [ ] Transcript saved
- [ ] ≥5 distinct pain points captured, each with hours/week
- [ ] Client hourly value captured (or estimable)
- [ ] Current tool stack + tech comfort noted
- [ ] Review call booked
- [ ] Transcript handed to Phase 2 (`.claude/skills/analyze-discovery-call`)
