# AI Concierge — Pre-Engagement Onboarding Form

Send before the first call (Google Form works fine). This is what makes the
engagement feel premium from day one — and question 10 is the one that powers
renewals.

## Business snapshot

1. Business name, what you do, and who your customers are.
2. Team size and who does what (roles, not names, is fine).
3. Which software tools does the business run on today? (CRM, email,
   accounting, project management, scheduling, industry-specific — list them
   all.)

## Current AI usage

4. Have you used ChatGPT, Claude, or similar? What for?
5. Have you (or anyone on the team) ever built an automation or AI workflow?
   What happened to it?
6. On a scale of 1–10, how comfortable are you learning a new tool with
   guidance?

## Pain and priorities

7. List the 3 tasks or processes that eat the most of your week right now,
   with a rough hours-per-week for each.
8. Which of those three, if it disappeared tomorrow, would change your life
   the most?
9. What's a process in your business that only exists in your head (nothing
   written down)?

## Defining the win

10. **90 days from now, what would make this engagement feel like a win?**
    Be specific — "I no longer personally do X," "the team handles Y without
    me," "Z takes 1 hour instead of 5."
11. What would make it feel like a waste of money?

## Logistics

12. Best days/times for our two monthly calls?
13. Preferred async channel for questions between calls (Voxer/text/email)?
    (Reminder: 12-business-hour response time.)

---

## Operator notes (don't send this part)

- Turn Q10's answer into the headline of the client's Notion one-pager. Track
  progress against it after every call.
- When the 90-day win is achieved (often inside 60 days), point at it
  explicitly — "when we started, you said X would be a win; we've passed it" —
  and open the next-tier conversation.
- Call 1 is foundation only: connect tools, context files, global
  instructions, show a scheduled task and a skill. Build nothing on call 1.
- Every later call: pick one workflow from Q7 and run **AOA** — Audit
  (show me how you do it), Optimize (13 steps → 7), Automate (make it a
  Claude skill).
- Update the accomplishments inventory in Notion after every call and email
  it to the client the same day.
