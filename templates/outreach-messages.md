# Outreach Scripts — All Seven Acquisition Methods

Copy, personalize, send. Never pitch in message one. Full strategy per method:
[docs/07-finding-clients.md](../docs/07-finding-clients.md).

---

## 1. AI Meetup — co-working space pitch

**Email/DM to the space manager:**

> Hi [Name] — I run [your brand], helping local businesses put AI to work. I'm
> organizing a free "AI for Business" meetup for [City] business owners and I'd
> love to host it at [Space]. I'll handle promotion and bring 25–30 local
> owners through your doors — great exposure for the space. Could I use your
> conference room for an hour on [two date options]?

**Follow-up to attendees (within 24 hours):**

> Great meeting you at last night's AI for Business meetup, [Name]. You
> mentioned [their pain point] — that's exactly the kind of thing I dig into in
> my AI assessment: 45 minutes together, and I come back prescribing 3–7 tools
> that reclaim at least 5 hours of your week, or it's free. Want me to send
> details?

## 2. Door knocking — opener

> "Hi, is the owner or manager in? ... Hey, I'm [Name] — I live here in [City]
> and I help local businesses like yours use AI to get hours back every week.
> I'm not selling anything today — I'd just love 15 minutes this week to show
> you one thing AI could take off your plate. Worst case, you learn a tool you
> hadn't heard of. When's a good time?"

## 3. LinkedIn DM — local owner

**Message 1 (probe, never pitch):**

> Hey [Name], I'm [You] — I probably live down the street from you here in
> [City]. Very curious: how are you using AI in [Business] right now, if at
> all? I work with local [industry] businesses on exactly this and I'd love to
> give you some free feedback — no strings.

**Message 2 (after they reply):**

> That's really common — most [industry] owners I talk to are in the same
> spot. Happy to record you a quick 2-minute video with two or three specific
> ideas for [Business] if you'd find that useful?

*(Voice notes and 30-second videos outperform text — almost no one sends them.)*

## 4. Free mini-audit — text to your network

> Hey [Name]! I'm getting into the AI services world. I'd love to spend 15
> minutes with you, completely free, and show you one way AI could benefit
> [their business]. Worst case you learn a tool or two you've never heard of —
> best case you've got a partner for all the AI stuff on your to-do list.
> Open to that?

## 5. Agency / professional partner

**Message 1:**

> Hi [Name] — quick thought. I know your clients must be asking you about AI
> constantly. I'm in the AI space (I do AI assessments and implementations for
> small businesses), and I'd love to be the resource you send those questions
> to. Happy to pay a referral fee for anyone who becomes a client. Coffee some
> time next week?

**The every-2–3-weeks follow-up (this is where referrals actually come from):**

> Hey [Name] — any of your clients asking about AI lately? Happy to jump on a
> call with any of them, no charge. Also had an idea: a co-branded workshop,
> "[AI for {their industry}] — brought to you by [Them] × [You]." Your clients
> get real value, you stay top of mind, I run the content. Interested?

## 6. AI office hours — co-working space pitch

> Hi [Name] — I'd love to offer your members something for free: once a week
> I'll set up in your space for an hour as an open "AI office hours." Any
> member can walk up and ask me anything about using AI in their business —
> free consulting for them, a nice value-add for [Space], zero cost to you.
> Could we trial it for a month?

*(Beast mode: door-knock nearby businesses for donated gift cards to raffle at
the session — "a way to get more people through your door.")*

## 7. Build in public — post formats

- **The win:** "A [industry] owner asked me [question]. Here's exactly what I
  told them: [answer]. [Result if any.]" — a win is smaller than you think.
- **The daily prompt (90-day play):** "The one [ChatGPT/Claude] prompt every
  [ICP] needs today: [prompt + what it does + example output]."
- **The teardown:** "I found 6 hours/week hiding in a [industry] business.
  Here's where it was: [pain point → tool, one per line]."
- **The assessment recap (with permission, anonymized):** "This week's
  assessment: [X] hours/week reclaimed for ~$[Y]/month in tools. The
  prescriptions: …"
